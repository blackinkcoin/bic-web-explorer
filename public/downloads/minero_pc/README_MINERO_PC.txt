================================================================================
          PC DESKTOP MINER - BLACK INK COIN (BIC)
================================================================================

This miner allows anyone with a PC (Linux, Windows, or macOS)
to participate in Layer 1 collaborative mining and earn BIC coins.

FEATURES:
- Proof of Human Touch through interactive spacebar / click verification.
- Democratic collaborative micro-shares without overheating your processor.
- Anti-Sybil protection with sub-linear IP distribution.
- Compatible with any public or local bicd node.

HOW TO RUN ON LINUX / MACOS:
1. Open a terminal in this folder.
2. Run:
   chmod +x start_miner.sh
   ./start_miner.sh

HOW TO RUN ON WINDOWS:
1. Double click on:
   start_miner.bat

CONFIGURATION IN MINER WINDOW:
- Paste your public shielded wallet address (starts with 'bic666_...').
- Set the node URL (default: http://127.0.0.1:6660 or your remote node IP).
- Click or hold Spacebar to refresh your 120s Proof of Human Touch window.
- Click "START CPU MINING".

You will begin submitting shares and receive payouts upon every sealed block!
